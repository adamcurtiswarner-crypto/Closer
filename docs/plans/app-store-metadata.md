## Description (4000 chars max)

One question a day. Both of you answer. Then you see what the other said.

Stoke is a daily ritual for couples who want to stay close — not through grand gestures, but through small, honest moments. Every day, you and your partner answer the same question, rate it on a scale, and add a note. Your answers are sealed until you both respond. Then you see each other.

What happens next is what makes Stoke different.

If you both score high, Stoke goes deeper — a follow-up question that builds on what is working. If either of you scores low, a gentle repair conversation arrives the next day. If your scores are far apart, a divergence question helps you understand what the other is seeing. No other app does this.

Over time, your answers build a living picture of your relationship across 12 dimensions — communication, intimacy, affection, money, family, friends, fun, future dreams, everyday life, conflict, appreciation, and growth. The Us view shows where you are close, where you differ, and which way things are moving. It is a mirror, not a report card.

What is included free, forever:
- A new question every day, sealed until both of you answer
- Your first follow-up conversation
- Three explore questions per month from 12 categories
- Reactions on every answer (Love, Spark, Smile, Moved)
- Your current month in the Hearth

What Premium unlocks:
- Unlimited follow-up conversations (deepener, repair, divergence)
- Full Hearth history and score trends
- The Us view — your alignment map across all 12 categories
- Unlimited explore questions to send your partner
- One subscription covers both of you

Stoke is not therapy. It is not a quiz. It is not a game. It is the daily practice of paying attention to the person you chose.

Warm. Quiet. Direct. No streaks. No guilt. No exclamation points.

Just the two of you, tending what matters.
