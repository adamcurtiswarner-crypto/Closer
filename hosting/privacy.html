Stoke

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Stoke is a relationship app that helps two partners stay connected through one question a day. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Stoke is operated by Omnific Collective, LLC (“Stoke,” “we,” “us”).

Plain-language summary: we collect the account and relationship details you give us, your answers to the daily question, and basic usage and diagnostic data. Your answers are shared with your partner — that is the point of the app. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we never send your answers to an AI service. You can export, anonymize, or delete your data from inside the app at any time.

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account: your email address and password, or the identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google if you use those.
  • Profile and relationship details: your display name, what you call your partner, and optional details such as anniversary date, relationship stage, love language, and the tone preference you set during onboarding.
  • Your answers: the daily questions you respond to, including your 1–10 rating, the written note you add, and any photo you attach.
  • Conversation around an answer: reactions, a clarify question you ask about your partner’s answer and their reply, and the questions you send each other from Explore.
  • Pairing: the invite code used to link you with your partner into a couple.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage events: first-party product events such as a question delivered, an answer submitted, a reaction added, or a follow-up completed. These are tied to your account and stay in our own systems.
  • Device and delivery: your timezone and locale (so “today” and the morning delivery are correct where you live) and a push token for the device you allow notifications on.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports, used to keep the app working.

What we deliberately do not collect

No location. No contacts. No calendar. No voice notes. No therapy, diagnosis, or trauma history. No children’s data. No payment card details — purchases run through Apple and we never see your card. No advertising identifiers, no session replay, and no third-party tracking pixels of any kind.

How your partner sees your information

Stoke is built for two people, so most of what you write is meant to reach the other one. It is worth being precise about which is which.

  • Sealed until you both answer: your rating, your note, and any photo on the daily question stay hidden from your partner until both of you have responded. Then both answers are revealed together.
  • Shared once revealed: ratings, notes, photos, reactions, clarify exchanges, and the “we talked about it” marks you make together.
  • Shared by design: your display name, anniversary, and love language — these exist so the two of you can see them side by side.
  • Private to you: how a reveal felt (warm, okay, hard) is never shown to your partner. It is used only to tune what the app offers you.

We cannot control what your partner does with something they can see. They can screenshot it, copy it, or repeat it outside the app. Please do not write anything in Stoke that you would not want the other person to keep.

How we use your information

To run the app and nothing else: delivering your daily question in your own morning, sealing and revealing both answers, deciding whether your two ratings call for a follow-up conversation (a deepener when you both scored high, a repair when either of you scored low, a divergence question when you scored far apart), building the shared history in the Hearth and the alignment picture in the Us view, sending the notifications you have allowed, keeping your subscription status correct, and fixing the app when it breaks.

We also look at aggregate product metrics — how many couples finish a question, where people stop — to decide what to build. We do not sell your data, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not build profiles of you for anyone else.

Artificial intelligence

Your answers are never sent to an AI service. We use Anthropic’s Claude for one job only: writing new questions for the library, from our own existing questions. That happens away from your account and sees none of your content — not your notes, not your ratings, not your clarify exchanges, not your photos.

If that ever changes, we will say so here before it does.

Safety detection

Stoke scans the text of answers and clarify exchanges for language that may signal a crisis — references to self-harm, abuse, or acute distress. The check runs on your device and on our servers. When something matches, the app quietly shows support resources and holds back the follow-up question for that prompt.

This is not reported to any outside party, and the matched text is not stored separately or sent anywhere beyond the couple. It is a lexicon, not a judgment, and it will miss things — please do not rely on it. Stoke is not therapy, counseling, or crisis intervention. If you or someone you know is in crisis, contact local emergency services or the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988 in the US).

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Stoke sends six kinds: your daily question is ready; your partner has answered and the reveal is open; a follow-up question is ready; your partner reacted to your answer; your partner asked or answered a clarify question; and a weekly note about the week you two had. Explore questions your partner sends you arrive as the second kind.

Delivery respects your local waking hours, and you can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your device settings. Signing out hands that device’s push token back, so it stops being addressable.

How we share information

With your partner, as described above. Otherwise only with the service providers that make the app work:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud — accounts, database, photos, server functions, and our own first-party analytics. Data is stored in the United States.
  • Expo Push Service and Apple Push Notification service — delivering the notifications above.
  • Apple App Store and RevenueCat — whether your subscription is active. Never your payment card.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — writing library questions only. Never your answers.
  • Sentry — crash and error reports.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety, or as part of a merger or acquisition — in which case this policy travels with the data, and we will tell you before it changes.

Security

Your data travels over TLS and is stored on Google’s infrastructure, encrypted at rest with Google’s standard encryption. Access is scoped per couple by server-side security rules, so one couple’s content is not reachable by another.

To be straight with you about what that does and does not mean: there is no additional field-level or end-to-end encryption. Your answers are stored as written text, and we could read them if we had reason to go looking. We do not, but a claim of end-to-end privacy would be false and we are not going to make one.

Retention and deletion

  • While your account is open, your answers and shared history are kept — the history is the product, and nothing quietly expires.
  • Delete your account from the app. It is deactivated immediately, and the permanent purge of your records runs automatically within 30 days.
  • Anonymize instead, if you want to keep the account but detach your past answers from your name.
  • Usage events move into our own analytics warehouse after 90 days and are kept in aggregate. Diagnostic error logs are deleted after 30 days.

If you unpair: either of you can end the couple without the other’s approval. Your former partner immediately loses access to your answers, your photos, and the shared history. They keep a read-only record that the relationship existed in the app, so their own account still makes sense to them; they cannot revive it or see anything inside it.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: export a copy of your data from the app, once per 24 hours.
  • Delete: delete your account from the app, as described above.
  • Anonymize: detach your past responses from your identity without deleting the account.
  • Unpair: end the couple connection at any time, with no approval needed from your partner.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or in your device settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under the GDPR (European Economic Area and UK) or the CCPA/CPRA (California), including the right to know, access, correct, delete, and — where applicable — object to or restrict certain processing, and the right not to have your personal information sold or shared (we do not sell or share it). Use the tools in the app or contact us below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

International users

We are based in the United States and our providers store and process data in the United States. If you use Stoke from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States.

Children

Stoke is for adults in committed relationships. You must be 18 or older to use it, and it is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When a change is material, we will update the date above and, where it matters, tell you in the app.
