6. How Stoke Works

Stoke delivers a daily question that both partners rate on a 1–10 scale and add a written note. Both answers are sealed until both partners respond, then revealed to each other. Based on your ratings, the app may trigger a follow-up conversation (a deepener when both scores are high, a repair when either is low, or a divergence conversation when scores are far apart).

You can also send your partner questions from an explore library of 12 categories, react to answers with emoji, and ask a clarify question about your partner’s answer. Stoke tracks your shared history in the Hearth, shows alignment trends across categories in the Us view, and may offer personalized suggestions while you wait for your partner to answer.

All of this information — scores, notes, reactions, clarify exchanges, alignment data — is visible to your linked partner.
